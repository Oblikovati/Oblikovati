# Dock and viewport appearance

The native desktop ran under Xvfb, Openbox, and Vulkan Lavapipe.
The build included the existing local file-system and dialog test edits.

- The active asset tab draws the 2 px brand underline. Clicking the other tab
  moves the underline and leaves the previous tab unmarked. [Asset dock](Appearance-Browser.png)
- The floating Material Browser uses its titlebar close control. Assigning and
  undoing a catalog material still works.
- The ViewCube label is legible in both Studio themes. [Light viewport](light-viewcube.png)
- Drawing sheets use white in Light and neutral white in Dark. The Part asset
  tabs are absent from the Drawing workspace. [Dark drawing](dark-drawing-paper.png)
